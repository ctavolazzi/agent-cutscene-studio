How to read it

Predicted level. Gain and pan from spatial.placeAt at the cue's place under LAWS[cue.law], heard from the camera playing at that moment. Modifiers (car rises out of the quiet, car swells as it comes in, wind eases, night opens) change the families they name from their onset by their track: the rise from its level up to 0, the others from 0 to their level, held after their end. The background playing is drawn at its own level; read a cue against it. Unplaced beds: no pan.
